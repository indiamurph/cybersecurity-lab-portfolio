## Key concepts

- **HDMI:** Carries digital video *and* audio on one cable. Other display connectors include DisplayPort, DVI (digital, sometimes analog) and VGA (analog only, legacy).
- **USB:** The standard interface for peripherals. It also supplies power, so keyboards and mice need no separate power source.
- **Power order:** Connect everything before powering on. Turning on the display first lets you see the POST and boot messages.
- **Front vs. rear view:** Being able to identify ports is a basic troubleshooting skill. Many "no display" tickets turn out to be a cable in the wrong port, such as onboard video instead of the graphics card.

## Takeaways

This is groundwork, but it matters at Layer 1. Most network problems are physical: a loose cable, the wrong port or no power. Building the habit of checking every connection end to end carries straight over to network troubleshooting.
